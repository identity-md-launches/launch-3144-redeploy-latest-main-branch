COURIER FIELD GUIDE / REV. 01

Your past is a tool.

01 / Move & record

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Tap Space or R to record a route. Travel through the cyan relay rings; tap again to release your echo. Each second recorded costs 9 energy.

02 / Let it replay

Your echo loops its route for 14 seconds. Only echoes charge relays: stay inside a ring for a moment. Charged relays stay online. Auditors prefer chasing echoes, giving you room to move.

03 / Claim & escape

Power every relay to unlock the amber vaults. Touch every vault to recover their memory. Then reach the green uplink to clear the sector and visit the upgrade shop.

04 / Stay intact

Red auditors and pulsing hazard tiles hurt. Shift dashes through danger. Collect ◆ fragments for upgrades and + repair chips for health. Energy regenerates when not recording.

Touch: hold the left movement pad; use REC, DASH and PAUSE on the right. P or Esc pauses. M toggles sound. Recording ends automatically when energy runs out.
